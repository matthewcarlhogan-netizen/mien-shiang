Terms of Service

These terms are a working draft. They set out the substance accurately, but they have not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be before the app is published. They are written as real terms rather than filler so that a review has something to correct.

1. What this app is

Mien Shiang shows you a face reading drawn from the Mian Xiang tradition, a traditional Chinese practice, together with measurements taken from a photo you supply. It is entertainment.

2. What it is not

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and it is not a substitute for professional advice of any kind.

It does not tell you facts about your character, your abilities, your future or your worth. Physiognomy — the belief that a face reveals character — has no scientific basis, and the app says so plainly on a screen reachable in one tap from every reading. Everything the reading says is presented as what a tradition holds, not as what is true of you.

It does not rate you. There is no attractiveness score, no ranking, and no comparison between people, by design.

3. Using the app

You may use it for personal, non-commercial purposes. Please do not:

  • use it to make decisions about other people — hiring, lending, renting, dating, or anything else that affects someone's life;
  • present its output as an assessment of anyone's character, ability or health;
  • upload photographs of people who have not agreed to it;
  • attempt to extract, retain or transmit the facial measurements the app computes.

4. Your photo

Your photo is processed on your own device and discarded. We never receive it. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

5. Availability and changes

The app is provided as-is. We may change or discontinue it. It needs to download a measurement library on first use; after that it works offline.

6. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any decision you make on the basis of a reading. The reading is traditional content offered for interest, and it is not advice.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under consumer law that cannot lawfully be excluded — including, in Australia, the consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law.
