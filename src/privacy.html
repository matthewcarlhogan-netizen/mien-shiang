Privacy Policy

Last updated: this policy describes the app as currently built.

The short version. Your photo is analysed on your device and then discarded. It is never uploaded or stored. The Qi Se tracker can keep derived reading values in this browser for your private history; they never reach us and can be deleted in one tap. The separate, optional device reminder keeps only its cadence, daytime preference and delivery-day state in a separate local database; it sends no reading content to us. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. We cannot see your face or your readings.

What the app collects

Camera and photos

When you take or choose a photo, it is drawn onto a canvas inside the app, measured, and discarded. The image, its pixels and the temporary face map exist only in the device's memory for the length of one reading.

None of the image, pixels or face map is transmitted or written to storage. The Qi Se tracker keeps only a small set of derived colour measurements, facial-proportion readings and categorical capture-quality fields in this browser, so it can compare today with your own previous readings. You can export or delete those local readings at any time. There is deliberately no upload endpoint, and an automated check runs on every build asserting that no network request can carry any value derived from your photo.

If you separately choose the optional screen-light experiment, the display slowly moves through neutral, blue and green light before the reading. Raw colour responses are discarded immediately. Only whether the check ran and whether its response was usable is kept with the local reading; it never identifies you and never blocks the reading.

Camera access is requested only when you first use it, after a consent screen that explains all of this. You can decline, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings. The screen-light experiment is off by default and has its own unchecked choice.

Optional device reminder

The Qi Se history screen offers a separate, default-off reminder. If you turn it on, the browser or installed app may show one neutral reminder during daytime hours, using the cadence and time you chose. The reminder says only “Today’s reading is ready.” It does not contain a face detail, measurement, heritage passage or personalised conclusion. The reminder preference and delivery-day record stay in a separate local database on this device; no server push subscription or message payload is created by this build.

You can turn the reminder off, pause it for seven or thirty days, or block notifications in your browser or device settings. Withdrawing Qi Se consent also deletes the reminder preference and delivery record.

Crash reports

Not currently active. This build contains no crash reporting and sends no crash data. The section below describes what would be collected if crash reporting is enabled in a future release, so you can see the intended limits in advance. This policy will be updated, and the change noted, before any such collection begins.

If crash reporting is enabled, it would collect: device model, operating system version, app version, and an anonymised stack trace describing where the app failed. It would not collect images, facial measurements, or any other data derived from your photo, and it would not collect anything identifying you.

Purchases

Not currently active. This build has no purchases, no subscriptions and no payment processing of any kind.

If purchasing is added, subscription status and purchase history would be handled by RevenueCat, and their handling would be governed by the RevenueCat privacy policy. Purchase data would never be combined with anything from your photo, because nothing from your photo leaves your device to be combined with.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics SDK of any kind.
  • No advertising SDK, no ad identifiers, no tracking pixels.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No third-party data sharing whatsoever in this build.
  • No sale of personal information, ever.

The MediaPipe face-measurement runtime and model are pinned parts of this app and load only from the same website as the scanner. The scanner makes no runtime request to a MediaPipe or model content network. These static files receive nothing about you, and after they are cached the app works entirely offline.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

The lawful basis for processing your facial image is your explicit consent, obtained on the consent screen before the camera is first used. You may withdraw consent at any time by revoking camera permission or deleting the app; withdrawal does not affect a reading already shown to you. Locally saved Qi Se readings can be deleted with the in-app “Withdraw consent and delete everything” action.

In practice there is very little for us to act on: we hold no personal data about you, so an access request will be answered truthfully with "we hold nothing about you". You may still make one, and we will respond.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

We do not sell or share personal information, and never have. Because no sale or sharing occurs, there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link to provide — that is the reason for its absence, not an oversight. Facial geometry is treated as sensitive personal information; it is processed solely to provide the reading you asked for, on your own device, and the geometry itself is not retained.

If you are in Australia (Privacy Act 1988)

The Privacy Act definition of sensitive information specifically includes biometric templates and biometric information used for automated verification or identification. This app does neither. It nevertheless treats the temporary face map conservatively: consent is obtained before processing, the map is destroyed after measurement, and the app keeps only derived colour and proportion readings needed for the private history.

There is no cross-border disclosure of biometric information, because that information never leaves your device in the first place. Local derived readings remain under your control in this browser and are not accessible to us.

Illinois residents (BIPA)

This section is provided as disclosure, not as a claim of exemption.

All facial analysis in this app happens on your own device. No biometric identifier or biometric information is transmitted to us or to any third party. Images and face maps are destroyed after measurement. The Qi Se tracker may retain derived colour and facial-proportion reading values in your browser's local database, but they are not accessible to us and can be deleted in the app. Nothing is linked to your identity, because the app has no accounts and collects no identifiers. We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric information.

Children

This app is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13. It is entertainment content about a traditional practice, and is not suitable as a source of guidance for anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app begins collecting anything it does not collect today — including enabling the crash reporting or purchasing described above — this policy will be updated before that collection starts, and the change will be noted in the app.
