On-device face scanner

Your face stays here

Before the scanner opens

This app measures the colour of a few areas of the face and describes what a classical Chinese tradition reads into them. It is for entertainment. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure anything, and it does not rate you.

What happens on this device. A 478-point map of your face is generated here, on this phone, purely to locate the areas being measured. It is used for the measurement and discarded immediately. It is never uploaded, never stored, and never linked to you. No photograph leaves this device, because no photograph is kept.

What is kept. Only the numbers: a handful of colour measurements per reading, so the app can compare today against your own previous readings. No image, no face map. One tap deletes all of it.

What this is not. The mapping between facial colour and anything else has no scientific basis that would support a health claim, and none is made here. Nothing in this app is a medical opinion or a substitute for one.

Why explicit consent is required

Australian Privacy Act: generating that face map counts as collecting sensitive information even though it is discarded milliseconds later, which is why this screen exists before the camera rather than after it. EU AI Act Article 50(3): this app categorises complexion, and this is the disclosure of that.
