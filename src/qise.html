Your face stays here

Your face stays with you.

During the scan. Your camera image or chosen selfie and a temporary 478-point face map are processed only on this device to find the areas being measured. Neither leaves the device: never uploaded, never stored. Both are discarded after the reading.

After the scan. Only small colour measurements and the reading stay on this device, so future scans can be compared with your own history. Delete everything at any time.

For entertainment and self-reflection only — not a medical or identity check.

Why explicit consent is required

The app temporarily generates a face map and new colour observations from your camera or chosen selfie. Even though the map is discarded immediately and nothing leaves this device, you should understand and choose that processing before it begins. The optional screen-light experiment is a separate choice below.

Camera fallback

Or choose a clear selfie

The photo is checked and measured only on this device, then discarded.

  • Face the camera straight on, at eye level, with your whole face visible.
  • Use soft daylight or a plain white light in front of you — not behind you.
  • Remove tinted glasses, hats and anything covering the eyes or cheeks.
  • Choose an original, unedited photo with no beauty filter or portrait blur.
