Before you begin

Read this. It changes what the results mean.

01
Your photo is analysed on this device and discarded straight afterwards. It is never uploaded, never stored, and never linked to you. The camera frame and the facial measurements taken from it are held in memory for the length of one reading and then thrown away.
02
This is entertainment. Mian Xiang (面相) is a traditional practice, and its core claim — that facial features reveal character — has no scientific basis. Read the output as tradition, not as fact about you.
03
It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Nothing here substitutes for a clinician. If a symptom worries you, see a doctor, and don't let anything this app says delay that.
04
It does not rate you. There is no attractiveness score, no ranking, and no comparison with anyone else — by design, and enforced by tests.
