Privacy Policy

Last updated: this policy describes the app as currently built.

The short version. Your photo is analysed on your device and then discarded. It is never uploaded, never stored, and never linked to you or to anything else. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. We cannot see your face, because it never reaches us.

What the app collects

Camera and photos

When you take or choose a photo, it is drawn onto a canvas inside the app, measured, and discarded. The image and the facial measurements taken from it (a set of coordinates describing the position of features, plus colour and texture values) exist only in the device's memory, for the length of one reading.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. None of it is written to storage. None of it is retained after the reading closes. There is deliberately no upload endpoint in the app, and an automated check runs on every build asserting that no network request can carry any value derived from your photo.

Camera access is requested only when you first use it, after a consent screen that explains all of this. You can decline, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

Crash reports

Not currently active. This build contains no crash reporting and sends no crash data. The section below describes what would be collected if crash reporting is enabled in a future release, so you can see the intended limits in advance. This policy will be updated, and the change noted, before any such collection begins.

If crash reporting is enabled, it would collect: device model, operating system version, app version, and an anonymised stack trace describing where the app failed. It would not collect images, facial measurements, or any other data derived from your photo, and it would not collect anything identifying you.

Purchases

Not currently active. This build has no purchases, no subscriptions and no payment processing of any kind.

If purchasing is added, subscription status and purchase history would be handled by RevenueCat, and their handling would be governed by the RevenueCat privacy policy. Purchase data would never be combined with anything from your photo, because nothing from your photo leaves your device to be combined with.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics SDK of any kind.
  • No advertising SDK, no ad identifiers, no tracking pixels.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No third-party data sharing whatsoever in this build.
  • No sale of personal information, ever.

The app does download two files from the internet on first use: the MediaPipe face-measurement library and its model file, from Google's and jsDelivr's public content networks. These are ordinary downloads of static files. They send nothing about you, and after the first load the app works entirely offline.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

The lawful basis for processing your facial image is your explicit consent, obtained on the consent screen before the camera is first used. You may withdraw consent at any time by revoking camera permission or deleting the app; withdrawal does not affect a reading already shown to you, because that reading exists only on your screen.

In practice there is very little for us to act on: we hold no personal data about you, so an access request will be answered truthfully with "we hold nothing about you". You may still make one, and we will respond.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

We do not sell or share personal information, and never have. Because no sale or sharing occurs, there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link to provide — that is the reason for its absence, not an oversight. Facial geometry is treated as sensitive personal information; it is processed solely to provide the reading you asked for, on your own device, and is not retained.

If you are in Australia (Privacy Act 1988)

Facial measurements are biometric information, which is sensitive information under the Australian Privacy Principles and attracts a higher standard of protection. Consent is obtained before collection, on the consent screen, and collection is limited to what the reading requires.

There is no cross-border disclosure of biometric information, because that information never leaves your device in the first place. You may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold; as above, we hold none.

Illinois residents (BIPA)

This section is provided as disclosure, not as a claim of exemption.

All facial analysis in this app happens on your own device. No biometric identifier or biometric information is transmitted to us or to any third party. Nothing is retained after a reading is displayed: there is no database, no storage layer, and no file written. Nothing is linked to your identity, because the app has no accounts and collects no identifiers. We do not sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric information.

Children

This app is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13. It is entertainment content about a traditional practice, and is not suitable as a source of guidance for anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app begins collecting anything it does not collect today — including enabling the crash reporting or purchasing described above — this policy will be updated before that collection starts, and the change will be noted in the app.
