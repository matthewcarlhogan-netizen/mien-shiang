# Acquisition task — Three Sections 三停 primary source

**Status: OPEN. Evidence status must not be upgraded until this is done.**
Raised 17 August 2026 under `DR-2026-08-17-B020-CLASS-A` (R1).

## The problem, stated exactly

The entire evidential basis for the Three Sections construct — for the idea that
balanced thirds are auspicious, which is the whole content of the reading — is
**one eight-character maxim**:

> 三停平等，富貴榮顯
> *When the three sections stand equal, wealth, rank and honour are manifest.*

What we actually have:

- The maxim is attributed to **麻衣神相** (*Mayi shenxiang* / *Mayi xiangfa*) by
  **two independently retrieved secondary sources**.
- The attribution of the *text itself* is legendary: 麻衣道者, "the Hemp-Robed
  Daoist", said to be Chen Tuan's teacher. **The historical existence of the
  figure and the date of the text are not established by anything retrieved.**
- **No Ming or Qing edition body text was retrieved.** ctext.org returned 403;
  shidianguji.com and baike.baidu.com are robots-blocked.
- 神相全編 — whether it contains a dedicated 三停 chapter — returned **conflicting
  evidence, unresolved**. Its table of contents does contain 十三部位總歌, which is
  a *different* facial-division scheme and must not be conflated.

So: a construct we ship, resting on one line, from a text of disputed
authorship, seen only through secondary sources.

**And the maxim itself is a wealth-and-rank claim** — prohibited output under
R11. We cite it as what a text says; we never render it as a reading about the
user. That makes the citation load-bearing in a second way: if the attribution
is wrong, we have quoted a fortune claim to no source at all.

## The task

Retrieve and check a **primary recension of 麻衣相法 / 麻衣神相** and establish
whether the maxim is in it, where, and in what wording.

### Acceptance criteria

1. A photograph or licensed digital scan of the page carrying the maxim, or a
   documented negative result — that a named recension does **not** contain it.
2. Edition identified: printer or 藏板, date, juan and folio.
3. The surrounding passage transcribed, so we can see whether the maxim is a
   heading, a verse line, or a commentator's gloss. **This matters:** a
   commentator's gloss is a different kind of claim from a text's own statement.
4. A note on whether the recension carries a 三停 section at all, or only the
   maxim in passing.

### Where to look

- **HathiTrust** holds catalogue records for *Shen xiang quan bian* (011737350)
  and *Liuzhuang shen xiang quan bian* (005119892). Both are Chinese-language
  copies, **not translations**. Access level per volume unchecked.
- **欽定古今圖書集成 藝術典 相術部** on Chinese Wikisource is PD-tagged and was
  retrievable for the Five Mountains and Four Rivers material. It is the first
  place to look for a 三停 passage, because it worked when the others did not.
- **A university East Asian collection** with a 麻衣相法 recension. This is the
  route that produces a citable folio.
- **ctext.org** holds the text but blocks automated retrieval and prohibits bulk
  download. Manual consultation under its terms is permitted; scraping is not.

### Who

A researcher with reading Chinese and library or licensed digital access. Not a
task that can be completed from an automated retrieval session — that has
already been tried and is what produced this document.

## What happens with each outcome

| Outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Maxim found in a datable recension, with folio | Requirement 1 closes for this family. Cultural review still required. |
| Maxim found only in a commentary layer | Ship it as a commentator's gloss and say so. The construct survives with a weaker, honestly stated basis. |
| Maxim not found in any recension checked | **The construct's stated basis is wrong.** Either find the real source or withdraw the auspiciousness claim and present Three Sections as a division of the face with no attached judgement. |
| No access obtainable | Status stays `Blocked`. Do not ship the family commercially. |

The third outcome is a real possibility and the plan should assume it. A
division of the face into three, presented without the fortune claim, is still a
legitimate heritage entry — it is the *claim* that needs the source, not the
division.
